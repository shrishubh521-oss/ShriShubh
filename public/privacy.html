ShriShubh – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 31 October 2026 · Last Updated: 31 October 2026

Current website features: ShriShubh currently provides public website information and a project-enquiry form, plus a restricted administrator dashboard. Customer self-registration, customer accounts, online ordering and online payment are not currently offered. References below to those features apply only if and when they are introduced.

1. Introduction

Welcome to ShriShubh. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal information. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, processed, protected and shared when you visit our website, contact us or use our services. Where consent is legally required, we will obtain it separately and appropriately.

2. Information We May Collect

Personal and enquiry information

  • Name and email address supplied in a project enquiry.
  • Phone number or business information only if you choose to provide it through a communication channel.
  • Project requirements, descriptions, preferences, support requests and correspondence.

Account information

Customer registration and login are not currently available. If introduced, we may process account identifiers, authentication information, preferences and session-related information. Authentication secrets should be handled by the authentication provider and not stored as readable passwords by ShriShubh.

Payment information

Online payments are not currently processed through this website. If payment facilities are introduced, authorized payment providers may process transactions. We do not intend to store raw card numbers or CVV values on ShriShubh systems. We may receive transaction references, payment status, amount and date.

Technical and security information

Hosting, database and security services may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device information, request metadata, and diagnostic or security logs where needed to operate and protect the website.

3. How We Use Information

  • Respond to project enquiries and discuss requirements.
  • Prepare quotations and provide requested website-development services.
  • Provide support and service-related communications.
  • Maintain website security, diagnose errors and prevent misuse.
  • Manage customer accounts, orders or payments only if those features are introduced.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and handle disputes.

We will not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis and notice.

4. Age Restriction and Children's Privacy

The public information pages of this website are not an adult-only service. ShriShubh does not currently offer customer account registration, online checkout or paid-service ordering through the website. If adult customer accounts or online paid-service facilities are introduced, those facilities will be intended for individuals aged 18 years or older. The minimum age will be clearly shown before registration or ordering, and an age-assurance method proportionate to the risk will be used. We will avoid collecting a full date of birth unless genuinely necessary.

We will not knowingly allow a person who declares that they are under 18 to create an adult customer account or proceed through an adult-only registration flow. If a future service is designed for children or processes children's personal data, ShriShubh will implement verifiable parental or lawful-guardian consent where required by applicable law before processing that data. A simple age checkbox will not be treated as a substitute for legally required verifiable parental consent.

We will not undertake prohibited tracking or behavioural monitoring of children or targeted advertising directed at children. If we identify children's personal data collected without a required lawful basis or consent, we will stop the relevant processing and take appropriate deletion or remediation steps, subject to legal retention requirements.

5. Data Protection and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information and services, which may include HTTPS, restricted database permissions, server-side authorization, role-based access controls, database Row Level Security, secure session handling, rate limiting and security logs. Public frontend configuration values are not secret; database policies and server-side controls must enforce access. No internet service can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell customers' personal information. Limited information may be shared with service providers necessary to operate the website, such as hosting, database/authentication, email, payment or AI providers if used. They should receive only information needed for their services and be subject to appropriate safeguards. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or lawful legal process.

7. AI Chat Support and Automated Services

If AI support is introduced, messages submitted to it may be processed by ShriShubh and the selected AI provider to generate responses. We will limit shared information to what is needed, restrict access to each customer's information, and apply appropriate processor and security safeguards. Do not submit passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary confidential information to a public chat interface. AI responses may be inaccurate and should not be treated as binding professional or contractual advice.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use essential technologies for functionality, preferences and security. Non-essential analytics or tracking will be disclosed and configured with consent where required. Browser settings may allow you to control cookies. We will not use tracking for prohibited behavioural monitoring of children.

9. Data Retention

Information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. Retention may depend on enquiry handling, service delivery, financial records, legal obligations, security and dispute resolution. Information no longer needed will be securely deleted or anonymized, subject to lawful retention requirements.

10. Privacy Rights and Requests

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request information about their personal data, correction of inaccurate data, erasure, withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent, grievance redressal and nomination rights. Requests may be sent to the contact email below. We may reasonably verify a request before acting and will respond within applicable legal timelines.

11. Data Breaches

We will investigate, contain and document personal-data incidents and provide notices to affected individuals and authorities as required by applicable law and within applicable timelines.

12. Third-Party Websites

Links to external websites are provided for convenience. ShriShubh does not control their privacy or security practices. Please review their policies before sharing information.

13. International Data Processing

Some service providers may process information outside India. Where applicable, ShriShubh will take steps to comply with Indian law, government restrictions and relevant contractual safeguards.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when services, technologies, data practices, legal requirements or security measures change. The latest version and revision date will be published here. Significant changes will be notified where required.
